Privacy policy for cartae.online

Version: November 2026 · applies to the website cartae.online

In short:

cartae.online needs no user account, sets no cookies, does no tracking and shows no advertising. You enter payment data only at Stripe; we do not receive it.

This page is the English version of the parts of our German privacy policy that apply to cartae.online. The complete German policy, which also covers driftware.online and its other offers, is available here (German).

1. Controller

Driftware, Nohlstr. 215, 46045 Oberhausen, Germany. E-mail: info@driftware.online. Cartae (cartae.online) is an offering of Driftware and is operated and maintained by Driftware.

2. What this page covers

This page describes the processing on cartae.online: books, free printable card decks, the daily card and the sample downloads. For the Cartae app there is a separate privacy notice for the app.

3. Data processing on cartae.online

cartae.online uses no user account, no cross-site tracking, no advertising SDKs and no cookies. No device identifiers or location data are collected. The card images, card decks (PDF), samples and the pages themselves are delivered by our own server without any additional connection to third parties; Cloudflare is placed in front (see below). Links to Amazon (printed books) are ordinary links: you leave our site only when you click them.

4. Hosting and protection against attacks (Cloudflare)

cartae.online is operated on Driftware’s own server in Germany. Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) is placed in front as a forwarding and protection service. When you visit the site, Cloudflare and our server process technically necessary connection data such as your IP address in order to deliver the page and protect it against attacks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure and reliable provision (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare’s privacy policy also applies.

5. Transfer to the USA

Some of the service providers mentioned (Cloudflare, Stripe) are based in the USA. A transfer of your IP address there relies on the European Commission’s adequacy decision on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, where the provider is certified under it, and otherwise on standard contractual clauses (Art. 45, 46 GDPR).

6. Browser storage (daily card)

The daily card is chosen in your browser. So that you see the same card all day, your browser stores the date and the number of the card locally (key “cartae-tageskarte”). This information does not leave your device and is required for the function you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, the German telecommunications and digital services data protection act). You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

7. Purchase of digital books on cartae.online

Which data: When you buy, we process your e-mail address, your name and your address as far as you provide them during checkout, the product bought, date and amount, the payment confirmation, and your consent to immediate delivery with the time of consent. You enter payment data (card, bank account or PayPal data) at Stripe; we do not receive it.

Purpose and legal basis: performance of the contract and delivery (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), compliance with tax and commercial-law retention obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR), proof of your consent under § 356(5) of the German Civil Code (BGB) and protection against abusive downloads (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Payment service provider Stripe: Payment is handled by Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., Ireland (with Stripe, Inc., USA, as a further processor). Stripe’s privacy policy applies, see stripe.com/privacy. Stripe also processes data for fraud prevention. For transfers to the USA Stripe relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

Delivery and e-mail: After payment, Stripe sends you back to cartae.online. There our own service checks with Stripe whether the payment has been received and provides the download. It stores only the order ID and the number of downloads. The service does not write IP addresses to its log; to protect against abuse it counts purchase requests per IP address in memory for one minute at most. We send the order confirmation with the download link through our e-mail provider (Namecheap, Private Email).

Retention: We keep invoice and booking data for the statutory periods (usually up to 10 years under § 147 of the German Fiscal Code (AO) and § 257 of the German Commercial Code (HGB)). We delete the download counters after one year at the latest. Without an e-mail address we cannot deliver.

8. Children

The offers on cartae.online are not aimed at children under 16. Individual technical requests may transmit IP addresses to the service providers named. We do not ask children to provide personal data.

9. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Because using individual functions can trigger technical connections to external service providers, it cannot be ruled out in general that personal data such as IP addresses are processed. Even though we ourselves store hardly any personal data, you can reach us at any time with requests about your rights at info@driftware.online. If you have consented to a processing, you can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. You can also object, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR). You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The competent authority is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia (Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen), Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213 Düsseldorf (ldi.nrw.de).

10. Changes to this policy

We reserve the right to adapt this privacy policy when necessary, for example when new apps or functions are added. The current version can always be found on this page.
