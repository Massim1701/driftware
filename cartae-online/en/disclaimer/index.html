Note on the cards

Version: November 2026 · applies to Cartae and the Lenormand and Tarot books and decks by Driftware

The cards are a companion, not a guide.

Cards cannot predict the future.

Do not run your life by the cards. A reading can be a hint, but it is not necessarily true.

The cards are a companion, not a guide. Use them to pause, reflect and ask yourself questions, not to hand over your decisions.

Never make important decisions, for example about health, money or legal matters, on the basis of a card reading alone, but with qualified advice. The readings do not replace medical, legal or financial advice.

All readings in books, decks, website and app are tendencies and prompts for reflection, not predictions and not statements of fact.

How it was made, and AI as a tool

The ideas, projects and content come from Driftware. We use AI like any other tool: to save time, to find and correct mistakes, and as support with texts, program code and images. Among others, Anthropic (Claude) supported us in part; the card images were generated with AI image tools. Selection, review and decisions are Driftware’s, as is the responsibility for the content. We say so openly, as newspapers and advertising do today.
