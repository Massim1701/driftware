Withdrawal information for digital content

Translation of the German information. The German version is legally binding.

Right of withdrawal

You have the right to withdraw from this contract within fourteen days without giving any reason. The withdrawal period is fourteen days from the day of conclusion of the contract.

To exercise your right of withdrawal you must inform us (Driftware, Nohlstr. 215, 46045 Oberhausen, Germany, e-mail: info@driftware.online) by means of a clear statement (for example an e-mail) of your decision to withdraw from this contract. You may use the model form below, but it is not mandatory. It is sufficient to send your notice before the withdrawal period has expired.

Effects of withdrawal

If you withdraw from this contract, we will reimburse all payments received from you without undue delay and at the latest within fourteen days from the day on which we received your notice. We use the same means of payment you used for the original transaction.

Early expiry of the right of withdrawal

For contracts for the supply of digital content not on a tangible medium, the right of withdrawal expires once we have begun to perform the contract after you have (1) expressly consented to us beginning the performance before the withdrawal period has expired, and (2) acknowledged that you lose your right of withdrawal by doing so (§ 356 (5) BGB). Both declarations are collected in the checkout.
