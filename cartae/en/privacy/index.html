Privacy for the app Cartae

Version: November 2026 · applies to the app Cartae (iOS and Android)

In short:

Cartae needs no account, shows no ads and does no tracking. Your questions, spreads and photos stay on your device. We never see them.

1. Controller

Driftware, Nohlstr. 215, 46045 Oberhausen, Germany. E-mail: info@driftware.online. The privacy policy of driftware.online (German) also applies to the website.

2. What the app stores on your device

  • Spreads and history: the cards you drew or laid and your readings, stored only locally. You can delete the history in the app at any time.
  • Settings, such as the language and the unlocked purchase status.

If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted. We do not keep a backup on our own servers.

3. Camera and photos

To recognise a row of laid cards the app asks for camera access. Without your permission the function stays off and everything else works. The photo is analysed on your device. It is not sent to us or to third parties and not stored permanently. You can withdraw permission at any time in your device settings.

3a. Reading aloud

The “read aloud” function uses the speech output of your device (iOS or Android). The text of the reading stays on your device. On some Android devices the speech output may use online voices depending on your settings; then the device’s speech service, not the app, sends the text to the provider. If you do not want that, choose an offline voice in your system settings. Your voice choice (female or male) is stored locally only.

4. No accounts, no ads, no tracking

  • No registration and no user account.
  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics or tracking services, no user profiles.
  • The app does not access location, contacts or microphone.

5. Purchase of the full version

The full version is processed through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play. You enter payment data only there; we do not receive it. See Apple’s or Google’s privacy policies for what they process. The app only checks whether the purchase exists in order to unlock the content.

6. Internet connections

The interpretations are stored in the app. Laying, drawing and reading need no internet connection. If you open a link in the app (website, free card decks, a book on Amazon) you leave the app and the privacy information of that site applies.

7. Content of the readings

Readings are tendencies and prompts for reflection, not predictions. They do not replace medical, legal or financial advice.

8. Children

Cartae is not aimed at children under 16. We do not collect data from children.

9. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Since we store no personal data from you through the app, there is usually nothing to disclose or delete. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority; for us this is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information North Rhine-Westphalia.

10. Changes

If we add functions that process data (for example an account or sync) we will update this page beforehand.
